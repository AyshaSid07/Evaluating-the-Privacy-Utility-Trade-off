## Prediction code for "Towards Understanding Fairness and its Composition in Ensemble Machine Learning", by Gohar et al.

Paper source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.04593

Original Github Repository: https://github.com/UsmanGohar/FairEnsemble

#### Contains four datasets:
- **Titanic ML:** Predicting passenger survival.
- **German Credit:** Predicting credit risk (good vs. bad loans).
- **Bank Marketing:** Predicting whether a client will subscribe to a term deposit.

*(All datasets are highly sensitive, real-world tabular benchmarks frequently used in algorithmic fairness literature. They contain prominent demographic protected attributes and quasi-identifiers such as Age, Sex, Marital Status, and Education level).*
